# IT Help FAQ

## How do I reset my VPN password?
Open the self-service portal at it.acme.test, choose "VPN", and follow the reset link sent to your work email. The new password must be at least 14 characters.

## How do I get access to a shared drive?
Ask the drive owner to add you, or open an IT ticket naming the drive and your manager.
