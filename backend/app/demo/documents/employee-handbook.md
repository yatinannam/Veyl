# Acme Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Acme's core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you may arrange your day with your team. Most teams meet in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Leave
Every employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave plus public holidays. Up to five unused days carry over to the next year. Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

## Travel and expenses
Book economy flights for trips under six hours. Hotels are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night, or 260 USD in high-cost cities. Submit receipts within 30 days of returning.

## Equipment
New starters receive a laptop and a monitor. Report lost or stolen equipment to IT within 24 hours.
