# Q3 Board Financials

Prepared for the board of directors. Restricted.

## Results
Acme's revenue in Q3 was 18.4 million USD, up 22 percent from Q3 last year. Gross margin was 71 percent. Operating loss narrowed to 1.2 million USD.

## Cash
Cash at the end of Q3 was 42.7 million USD, giving about 31 months of runway at the current burn rate.

## Outlook
The board approved a Q4 revenue target of 20.1 million USD and a hiring plan of 14 people. A price increase of 8 percent for new contracts takes effect on 1 January.
