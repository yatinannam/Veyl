# Engineering Roadmap: next two quarters

## Project Lighthouse
Project Lighthouse replaces the legacy billing engine. The new engine goes to internal beta in Q4 and to all customers in Q1. The team grows from six to nine engineers.

## Platform reliability
We will move the remaining services to the shared Kubernetes cluster, target 99.95 percent availability for the customer API, and retire the old monitoring stack by the end of Q4.

## Hiring
Open roles: two backend engineers, one site reliability engineer, and one engineering manager for the payments group.

This roadmap is confidential until it is announced at the all-hands meeting.
